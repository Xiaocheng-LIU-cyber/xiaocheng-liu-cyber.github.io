Master's Research

Triplane-Guided Sparse FlexiCubes for High-Resolution 3D Clothed Human Reconstruction

The approach combines triplane RoI features, hierarchical sparse voxels, and FlexiCubes to reconstruct clothed-human geometry at high resolution without using a dense high-resolution voxel grid.

Method

Overview

Starting from one RGB image, a diffusion stage provides orthographic views and camera coordinate maps (CCMs). U-Net features produce triplane representations, which support coarse 128³ geometry prediction and subsequent sparse refinement.

Triplane RoI sampling

Each candidate voxel projects to rectangular RoIs on the XY, XZ and YZ feature planes. A 2 × 2 grid is sampled from each RoI using bilinear interpolation.

With 32 channels per plane, the three RoIs yield 384 features. Combining them with a 24-dimensional positional encoding gives a 408-dimensional feature vector for local geometry prediction.

Sparse voxel refinement

128³

Global coarse field and initial FlexiCubes mesh.

256³

Subdivide active parent voxels; query finer features and prune candidate support.

512³

Refine active surface regions and extract the final sparse mesh.

Subdivision expands selected voxels into 2 × 2 × 2 children. Maintaining sufficient neighboring active voxels is important for FlexiCubes connectivity.
