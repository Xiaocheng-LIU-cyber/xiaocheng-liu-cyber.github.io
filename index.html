Methodology

Pipeline · RoI Features · Sparse Refinement

Our framework combines multi-view image conditioning, a learned triplane representation, and a coarse-to-fine voxel hierarchy for high-resolution clothed-human mesh reconstruction. It uses a dense low-resolution stage for global shape, followed by surface-focused sparse refinement.

1. Reconstruction Pipeline

Starting from a single RGB image, a diffusion-based stage provides orthographic RGB images and camera coordinate maps (CCMs). U-Net features form three orthogonal triplane feature maps. RoI feature queries support a dense 128³ voxel prediction and initial FlexiCubes mesh extraction. The coarse surface then provides a starting region for sparse voxel refinement toward a 512³-resolution mesh.

2. Triplane RoI Feature Sampling

Project each voxel region. A candidate 3D voxel is mapped to a rectangular RoI on each of the XY, XZ, and YZ triplane feature maps. This captures the projected extent of the voxel rather than querying only a single plane pixel.

Sample and fuse features. In the current implementation, a 2 × 2 feature grid is bilinearly sampled from each plane's RoI. With 32 feature channels per plane, the three planes yield 384 features; concatenating 24-dimensional 3D positional encoding produces a 408-dimensional feature vector.

Predict local geometry. The fused RoI features condition subsequent geometry predictions at the queried spatial locations, including signed-distance and deformation estimates used by FlexiCubes.

Three feature planes Bilinear RoI sampling Spatially aligned queries

3. Coarse-to-Fine Sparse Voxel Upsampling

Here, “upsampling” means subdividing the spatial voxel grid and refining its active support near the reconstructed surface. It is distinct from simply enlarging a 2D triplane image or allocating a fully dense 512³ voxel volume.

128³

Global initialization

Predict a dense coarse voxel field and extract an initial FlexiCubes mesh. Identify candidate near-surface voxels to form the sparse starting support.

256³

Subdivision and pruning

Subdivide selected parent voxels into 2 × 2 × 2 children, sample triplane RoI features for finer locations, and prune candidate voxels to keep refinement sparse.

512³

Fine geometry and meshing

Repeat sparse subdivision and local feature queries at finer resolution; use sparse FlexiCubes to extract the high-resolution clothed-human mesh.

The intended advantage is to focus high-resolution computation around the surface. Maintaining adequate active-voxel neighborhoods is important for FlexiCubes, and aggressive pruning can remove regions needed for mesh extraction.
